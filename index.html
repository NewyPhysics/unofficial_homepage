Physics at the University of Newcastle, Australia

The Physics discipline is part of the School of Science in the College of Engineering, Science and Environment at the University of Newcastle, Australia. This unofficial website brings together our research and teaching in one place, so you can spend less time searching and more time doing physics.

For official University information, please visit the University of Newcastle website.
