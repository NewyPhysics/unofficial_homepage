title: Medical Physics

Medical physics research at Newcastle focuses on making radiation therapy for cancer more accurate and safer. The Medical Physics Research Group works closely with the Department of Radiation Oncology at Calvary Mater Newcastle, so research moves quickly from the lab into clinical practice.

Research topics include:

- **Real-time treatment verification:** using images taken with the treatment beam during radiotherapy to check, as it happens, that the dose is being delivered as planned. The group developed the world's first real-time treatment verification system of this kind.
- **Dosimetry:** measuring and reconstructing the radiation dose delivered to patients, including for moving tumours in the lung and liver, and running national and international dosimetry audits that help standardise practice and support clinical trials.
- **MRI in radiotherapy:** MRI-only treatment planning, AI-generated synthetic CT images, and functional and metabolic MRI to assess how tumours respond to treatment.
- **New treatment techniques:** evaluating advanced delivery methods such as stereotactic radiosurgery and the latest linear accelerator techniques, with industry partners.
- **Machine learning and Monte Carlo simulation:** detecting treatment errors automatically, and modelling radiation transport to design new detectors and improve dose calculations.
