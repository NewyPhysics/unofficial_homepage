Research projects on offer in the Physics discipline for {{ data.year }}, for summer research, SCIE3500 and Honours students.

We encourage undergraduates to get involved in research throughout their degree. Along the way you will learn to find, assess and use scientific sources, manage a project, carry out experimental or computational research, and present your results clearly, both in writing and in person. These skills give you a strong foundation for a career in industry, business or academia.

Ways to get involved

Summer research projects

Short, paid undergraduate research projects over the summer break.

SCIE3500

A 10-unit undergraduate course built around a research project with an academic supervisor, assessed on a progress report, research notebook, final report and oral presentation. Open to third-year students who have completed at least 140 units with a cumulative GPA of at least 5.0. Offered in both semesters and the winter term.

SCIE3500 course details

Honours

A full-year research project after completing the Bachelor of Science or another related degree, split 50% coursework and 50% research. A minimum GPA of 5.0 is required for entry.

Bachelor of Science (Honours)

Talk to a supervisor first. Always discuss a potential project with the supervisor before you enrol or apply. Supervisors are listed in alphabetical order, and you're welcome to contact them by email or drop by.
