Want a career in physics? Come and ask two recent Newcastle physics graduates how they got where they are, and how you can too. Wednesday 28 October 2026, 12:00–13:00, in PG08.

## Meet the panel

- **Georgia Fardell**, Data Analyst at Quantium
- **Lachlan Adams**, Physicist at Defence Science and Technology

## Bring your questions

- What is it like working as a physics graduate?
- How do you start your career?
- Which courses should you take?
- What salary can you expect?
- How do you break into industry or the public sector?

Everyone is welcome, from first-years to Honours and PhD students. Curious about where a physics degree can lead? See [where physics can take you]({{ '/teaching/careers/' | relative_url }}).
